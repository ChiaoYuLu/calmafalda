Mafalda Amaro

I am a fascinated-by-dogs person who has received Nordic Education Centre for Ethical Dog Training 's Certificate of Professional Ethical Dog Trainer by The Calming Signals Approach® and The Caring Human Code®.

Currently enrolled in this school to obtain my diploma as a dog behaviour specialist and following a course by Dr. Amber Batson on Juvenile Dog Behaviour.

Following ethical force-free training methods, science based communication and enriched environments, I am set to help dogs and their caregivers achieve harmony through understanding and respect.

Up to recently, I've also been a regular volunteer at the local shelter, where I focused on reducing stress levels and providing dogs with time for choice, exploration and learning to cope with their surroundings.

This is a journey to be continued, a life dream! I’m ready to share what I’ve learnt so far and invite you to discover fascinating details that people often miss and make the whole difference for the better.
